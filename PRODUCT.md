# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- English- and Korean-speaking visitors seeking accessible Christian Scripture for North Korean readers.
- Churches, missionaries, youth pastors, prayer partners, volunteers, and publishing partners evaluating or supporting Pyongyang Bible Institute's work.

## Product Purpose

Pyongyang Bible Institute publishes Bible translations and teaching resources in modern North Korean diction. The website explains why a distinct North Korean translation matters, makes current publications discoverable, documents the institute's history, and gives visitors clear paths to read, pray, support, or make contact.

## Positioning

PBI's defining work is an English–North Korean bilingual Bible translation that replaces unfamiliar Sino-Korean and South Korean wording with modern North Korean diction while closely following the New Living Translation.

## Operating Context

The public website is an informational and ministry site. Visitors arrive from search, partner referrals, Bible.com/YouVersion, churches, and PBI communications; most content is read without an account.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the public Squarespace site's ten-page content structure and its established `/en/...` URLs.
- Use the supplied local image and video assets rather than depending on Squarespace's CDN.
- Build in TypeScript for GitHub source control and Vercel deployment.
- Newsletter and contact delivery providers are not supplied; the migration must offer honest contact paths without inventing an integration.
- Public Bible content is described by PBI as royalty-free for publishing partners; this does not imply a general license for unrelated site assets.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name “Pyongyang Bible Institute” and the PBI logo.
- Retain the existing sky-blue, black, and white identity, documentary photography, bilingual English/Korean content, and grounded ministry voice.
- Avoid invented impact statistics, testimonials, partners, donation claims, or publication availability.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current public site: `https://www.pybible.org/` and its sitemap.
- Supplied logos, book covers, photographs, videos, home feature art, and favicon in `assets/`.
- Current public copy, outbound publication links, YouVersion links, legal designation, EIN, and contact details.

## Product Principles

1. Make the translation's purpose understandable before asking for support.
2. Let real publications, bilingual passages, and documentary material provide proof.
3. Preserve working URLs and make the site easy to scan on mobile.
4. Keep claims and calls to action faithful to PBI's supplied public record.
